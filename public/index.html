In design

Honesty as architecture

Claims trace to artifacts

Every claim traces to a frozen artifact. A spine script recomputes each headline number from raw data; if the script fails, the number does not ship.

Failures are reported

Negative and uncatchable results are reported as results — never hidden. The Qafiya paper's zero-recall classes are the template.

Limits are stated

Each paper states what its system provably cannot do — measured where possible, not guessed.

Where it fits

Anywhere an AI touches consequential data or decisions, the question stops being "do you trust it?" and becomes "show me."

AI sales agents

When an agent quotes a price, grants a discount, or makes a promise, each claim either traces to the catalog, the rules, and the conversation — or it is flagged. Providers get reports their customers, regulators, and courts can trust.

Document-heavy bureaus

Passport and visa data entry with zero silent errors: every extracted field is verified against the document's own checksums, and mismatches go to a human before anything is submitted.

Any consequential AI

Routing, memory, actions — all logged, all traceable. The OS turns "trust us" into "here are the receipts."
