Accountability as architecture

Claims trace to artifacts

Every claim traces to a frozen artifact. A spine script recomputes each headline number from raw data; if the script fails, the number does not ship.

Failures are reported

Negative and uncatchable results are reported as results — never hidden. The Qafiya paper's zero-recall classes are the template.

Limits are stated

Each paper states what its system provably cannot do — measured where possible, not guessed.

What it delivers

Wherever AI touches consequential data or decisions, DeeWaanAI OS delivers the same three guarantees.

Every decision, traceable

The OS records what the AI did, on which data, under which rules. When something goes wrong, you can show exactly what happened and why.

Every claim, grounded

Claims trace to their source data or are flagged before they reach a customer. Invented facts do not slip through silently.

Every record, verifiable

Hash-chained history means anyone can re-run the checks and get the same result — reports your customers and regulators can trust.
